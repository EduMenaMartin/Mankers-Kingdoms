# ADR-0001: Working title "Petty Kingdoms"

**Status:** Superseded by ADR-0016
**Date:** 2026-07-02
**Deciders:** Edu + Claude

## Context

Every project needs a name to write into documentation, folder names, code namespaces, and conversation. Waiting for the "perfect" title before starting is a form of procrastination. A working title lets work proceed; the shipping title can be decided later.

Candidate names considered included: Warden, Hearthfall, Keepshade, Everhold, Runemark, Foundling, Bannerholds, Ember & Stone, The Long Reign, Wardens of the Waste, Petty Kingdoms, Fiefdom, Bastion, The Marches, Marchlands, Holdfast, Of Crown and Coin, Blade & Banner, Kith & Keep.

"Petty Kingdoms" is a real historical term for early medieval kingdoms. Genre-literate players immediately recognize the reference. No commercial PC game currently ships under this title on Steam.

## Decision

Adopt "Petty Kingdoms" as the working title. Trademark search deferred to before commercial release. Subtitle to be added at 1.0.

## Consequences

**Positive:** clear working name, immediately evocative to target audience.
**Negative:** the phrase is a common historical term, so not defensible as trademark alone — will need a subtitle for commercial identity.
**Neutral:** rename cost is small; namespace refactor is a global search-replace.

## Alternatives considered

- "Kith & Keep" — memorable, unique, captures companions + settlement, but less genre-signaling.
- "Bannerholds" — evokes coop and settlements clearly.
- Others in the candidate list — each rejected on distinctiveness or memorability.
